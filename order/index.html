How it works

From photo to real night sky, in four steps.

1

Upload a photo

A portrait, a pet, a skyline — anything with a clear outline works well as a line drawing.

2

Outline

We create an outline based on your image.

3

Poster

The outline is matched to real stars in your chosen hemisphere, so every line on the poster is a real star in the real sky — delivered as a print-ready PDF, or a framed print to your door.

4

See your constellation live
