2. What we sell

A personalized "constellation portrait": a photo you provide is traced into a line drawing, matched against real star positions from an astronomical catalog, and delivered either as a print-ready PDF or as a physical framed print. Every poster includes a QR code linking to a companion app that helps you locate the constellation in the real night sky.

The star positions used are real. The fit between your drawing and those positions is a best-effort match, not a perfect trace — we don't guarantee an exact reproduction of your original image, and the final artistic result is at our discretion within that constraint.

Refund Policy

Every poster is made specifically for you, so the usual "change of mind" return rules that apply to off-the-shelf goods don't apply here in the same way — but mistakes on our part are always made right.

1. Before we start production

You can cancel and get a full refund any time before we begin producing your poster (tracing your sketch and matching it against the sky) — just email us. Once production has started, see below.

2. Once production has started

Because every poster is custom-made to your own photo and specifications, it isn't eligible for a "change of mind" refund once production has begun — this reflects the standard exemption for personalized/made-to-order goods under EU consumer law (and the equivalent position in most other jurisdictions), rather than a policy specific to us.

This doesn't affect your right to a refund or replacement if something's actually wrong with your order — see below.

Contact michael@almenr.dk with your order number and a photo of the issue where relevant, and we'll sort it out.
